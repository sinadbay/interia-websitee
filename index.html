About Interia

Pioneering the Future of Universal VR Transformation

Our Mission

At Interia, we're revolutionizing how people interact with 2D data by transforming it into immersive VR experiences. From floor plans to educational content, we make any data come alive in virtual reality.

The Problem We Solve

Traditional 2D data visualization limits understanding and engagement. Our VR platform transforms any 2D data into immersive experiences, whether it's architectural plans, educational content, or business data.

Our Vision

To make immersive VR experiences the standard way people interact with data across all industries - from education and architecture to business analytics and beyond.
