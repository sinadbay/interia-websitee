About Interia

Revolutionizing Real Estate Visualization

Our Mission

At Interia, we're transforming how people experience pre-construction real estate. We take your architects' 3D models and convert them into immersive VR experiences within just two weeks.

The Problem We Solve

Traditional property visualization leaves buyers uncertain. Our VR experiences let clients walk through their future property, explore layouts, and customize finishes in real-time.

Our Vision

To make immersive VR walkthroughs as standard as a floor plan in every property brochure, starting with the GCC's $5+ billion interiors market.

Our Services

From Concept to Immersive Reality

3D Model Conversion

Transform your existing architectural 3D models into VR-ready experiences with optimized performance and stunning visuals.

Immersive VR Walkthroughs

Create fully interactive VR experiences where clients can explore every corner of their future property with realistic lighting and materials.

Real-time Customization

Enable clients to customize finishes, colors, and materials in real-time, helping them make confident decisions before construction begins.

Sales & Marketing Tools

Powerful VR presentations for sales offices, exhibitions, and marketing campaigns that give you a competitive edge.
